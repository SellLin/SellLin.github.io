About us

YIDAO Singapore Technology Co., Ltd. was registered and established in Singapore in 2019. We are a high-tech enterprise specializing in product research and development, industrial design and global marketing.

Our values

We pursue forward-looking technological innovation. Our research balances current application needs with future development trends, helping build a smarter, more connected and sustainable intelligent ecosystem.

YIDAO has established a global industrial network with manufacturing bases in North America, Europe, Thailand and China.

We also operate a worldwide technical service network with local engineering teams, providing reliable after-sales support and ensuring stable operation of customer equipment across global markets.
